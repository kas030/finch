Third-party code retains its upstream licenses and notices.

third-party/sources.json records the source URL, pinned revision and SHA-256
of supplemental license texts missing from some Cargo source distributions.
Packaging scripts verify these bytes and collect the target's Cargo dependency
licenses plus the bundled PDFium archive's LICENSE and licenses directory.
The generated inventory includes build/test dependencies and is not a list of
only the code linked into the final executable.

Some historical MIT-licensed packages lack a standalone upstream copyright
statement. Their directories retain a NOTICE explaining the known author and
the use of SPDX's standard MIT template. Template placeholders are original;
they are not an assertion of a fabricated copyright year or holder. Preserve
these notices and review the corresponding upstream materials for distribution.

Each dependency is governed by its own license. These supplementary materials
do not replace the upstream copyright statements or grant additional rights.
